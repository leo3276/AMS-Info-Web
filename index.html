How it works

Three steps, in this order

  1. 01

    Everything lands in one place

    Invoices sent, expenses logged, bank transactions synced all of it flows into a single ledger instead of five separate tools.

  2. 02

    The system does the accounting

    Entries get categorized, invoices get tracked to paid or overdue, and the numbers stay current without a monthly close.

  3. 03

    Everyone who needs it, sees it

    The owner, a co-founder, and an accountant all look at the same real numbers reports, forecasts, and all at the same time.
